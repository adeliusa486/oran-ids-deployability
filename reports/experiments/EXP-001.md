## Research question

Does NetsLab-5GORAN-IDD actually contain what its descriptor implies, at the
granularity our split design (A4) and windowing policy (A12) require?

## Hypothesis (pre-registered in the registry, before download)

**H0-data:** the corpus exposes a per-record device or run identifier, a usable
timestamp, and CU–DU alignment sufficient to join flow records to radio telemetry.

**Result: H0-data is PARTIALLY FALSIFIED.** The first two hold. The third does not.

## Pre-registered failure criteria, and what happened

| Criterion, written before the download | Outcome |
|---|---|
| No usable group key → D-001 reverses, fall back to plan option A1-c | **Not triggered.** A group key exists |
| CU–DU join impossible → **claim C5 is withdrawn, not weakened** | **TRIGGERED** at the level of the published summary artefacts. See §4 — a route survives, but it costs a decision reversal |

## 2. Radio layer — `Lower_Layer_Data.db`

45,244 records, 25 columns, one table. **Zero exact duplicate rows.** Only `rnti` has
nulls (3.40%).

### Time axis — a better result than expected

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Epoch unit | milliseconds |
| Span | 2025-05-09 14:05:06 → 2025-07-01 13:09:17 (**52.96 days**) |
| Median inter-record gap | **1.000 s** (p95 1.000, p99 1.001) |
| Implied sampling rate | **1.0 Hz**, highly regular |

Two consequences the plan did not anticipate:

1. A 16-record window (A12) is **16 seconds** of radio telemetry, not an abstract
   count. That should be stated as a duration in the paper.
2. A 53-day span makes **time-disjoint splits and genuine drift analysis (Phase 12)
   feasible on real elapsed time.** This is a substantive capability gain.

### Labels

| Benign | 10,664 | 23.57 |
| Probe | 8,445 | 18.67 |
| DoS | 4,721 | 10.43 |
| Web Attacks | 3,273 | 7.23 |
| BruteForce | 3,057 | 6.76 |

### Group keys (A4) — the paper's stated split protocol is not supportable

The manuscript says `D_A` splits are "disjoint by device identifier".

| Candidate | Distinct | Largest share | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `cellid` | 1 | 100% | **TOO FEW** — single cell |
| `ue_id` | 9 | 60.8% | **TIGHT** and imbalanced — one UE is 61% of the data |
| `rnti` | 45 | 10.9% | numerically OK, but RNTIs are **reassigned**, so this is not a stable device identity |
| `attack_subcategory` | 19 | 23.6% | OK numerically, but grouping on it is **attack-disjoint**, a different and much harder protocol |
| **`session` (recovered, §2.4)** | **30** | **12.7%** | **OK — this is the usable key** |

**Device-disjoint splitting at 5 folds is not possible on this corpus.** With 9 UE
identifiers, one holding 61% of records, a device-disjoint 5-fold split produces wildly
unbalanced folds. The manuscript's stated protocol must change to **run-disjoint**,
which is also what the nearest prior work (Fard et al., IEEE CSR 2026) does on this
corpus.

### 2.4 Session recovery — a real run identifier exists

Segmenting the time axis at a 300 s idle gap yields **30 capture sessions**, and
**all 30 are label-pure** (each contains exactly one attack category).

| Idle-gap threshold | Sessions |
|---|---:|
| 30 s | 48 |
| 60 s | 46 |
| **300 s** | **30** |
| 900 s | 21 |

Label purity at 100% is the evidence that these are genuine capture runs rather than an
artefact of the threshold: an arbitrary segmentation of a mixed stream would not produce
30 consecutive pure blocks. Sessions per category: Benign 10, DDoS 6, Web 5, BruteForce 3,
DoS 3, Probe 3. Median 1,009 records per session (min 47, max 5,727).

`results/EXP-001/processed/radio_sessions.csv` lists them.

### 2.5 Windowing (A12) — and the sample-size problem

Grouping by `(session, ue_id)` with `min_records=16, short_flow_policy=drop`:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| Groups | 48 |
| Groups with ≥16 records | 48 (100%) |
| **Complete 16-record windows** | **2,808** |
| Records dropped by the policy | 316 (**0.70%**) |

A 0.70% drop rate is small and reportable, which resolves A12 cleanly.

**But 2,808 windows is a small dataset.** Under run-disjoint 5-fold splitting the
training set is roughly 2,200 windows of shape 16×22. This has a direct and unwelcome
consequence for the baseline ladder: a Transformer or a deep LSTM on ~2,200 sequences
is not a defensible modelling choice, and if we run one it will be for completeness,
not because the data supports it. This should be stated as a property of the corpus,
not discovered later and explained away.

## 4. Main result — the CU/DU record-level join is not possible

Tested from strongest join to weakest (`scripts/exp001_join_analysis.py`):

| Level | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **L0** | shared record-level identifier | **NOT POSSIBLE.** Radio has `ue_id`, `rnti`, `cellid`; network has `uid`, `src_ip`, `dst_ip`, ports. The intersection is empty |
| **L1** | shared time axis | **NOT POSSIBLE.** Radio has `timestamp`; the network CSV has **no time column at all**. Zeek's `ts` was dropped when the summary was built |
| **L2/L3** | run or category level | **POSSIBLE**, but only through a hand-written mapping: the two layers share just `attack_category` and `traffic_type`, with different spellings (`dos` vs `DoS`, `web` vs `Web Attacks`) and different granularity (19 radio subcategories against 15 network attack types, with **zero exact string overlap**) |

**Verdict: L2/L3 — run- or category-level association only.**

### The two layers are not aligned samples of the same events

| Category | Radio % | Network % | Difference (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| dos | 10.43 | 36.69 | **26.26** |
| benign | 23.57 | 9.91 | **13.66** |
| web | 7.23 | 16.72 | 9.49 |
| ddos | 33.34 | 24.38 | 8.96 |
| probe | 18.67 | 10.63 | 8.04 |
| bruteforce | 6.76 | 1.66 | 5.10 |

Attack prevalence differs by 13.7 points (76.43% radio, 90.09% network). Even a
category-level join would be joining populations with materially different composition.
This is expected — one row is one second of radio telemetry, the other is one flow — but
it means "multi-modal" in this corpus describes *parallel collection*, not *record-level
correspondence*.

## 5. Effect on claim C5

C5 requires a joined feature vector containing both radio and network families. From
the published summary artefacts it **cannot be evaluated**. Three routes:

| Route | Cost | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** Re-extract from the raw per-category archives. Both `.pcap` and the per-category `.txt` radio files carry timestamps, so an L1 time join is recoverable | download 16.4 GB, run one exporter, build the join | **C5 survives intact.** Requires reversing D-004, which the §1 correction already indicates on independent grounds. Prior work (Fard et al.) fuses these modalities, which is evidence the route works |
| **(b)** Restrict C5 to run-level late fusion | cheap | A **different and weaker claim**. It must be renamed, not quietly relabelled — run-level fusion cannot show that radio features are deployment-specific *within* a flow |
| **(c)** Withdraw C5 | free | The pre-registered failure criterion. Honest, and the paper survives on RQ1–RQ3 |

**Recommendation: route (a).** It is the only one that preserves the claim, it is
affordable now that the size is known, and it is independently supported by the §1
correction. Route (b) must not be adopted silently; if chosen, C5's text changes.

## 6. Effect on decision D-004

D-004 chose "published features on both sides" on the stated premise that `D_A` raw was
~1.5 TB and single-exporter processing was infeasible. **Two findings now bear on it:**

1. The premise was wrong. The raw data is 16.4 GB — roughly one evening's download and
   ordinary single-machine compute.
2. Independently of cost, the published summary artefacts **cannot support C5 at all**,
   because they cannot be joined. The cheap option does not merely weaken a claim; for
   C5 it removes the possibility of evaluating it.

D-004 is the owner's decision and stands until the owner changes it. It is flagged for
revisit with these two findings attached.

## 7. Unexpected results

1. **A 53-day time span with clean 1 Hz sampling.** Neither the plan nor the descriptor
   landing pages mentioned this. It makes time-disjoint splitting and real drift
   analysis possible, which upgrades Phase 12 from speculative to grounded.
2. **Sessions are recoverable and 100% label-pure.** A real run identifier can be
   reconstructed from the time axis alone.
3. **The corpus is small where it matters.** 2,808 radio windows against 1.7 M network
   flows — a 600× asymmetry between the two modalities.
4. **A labelled category with no published raw capture** (Probe).
5. **Device-disjoint splitting, the protocol the manuscript states, is not supportable.**

## 9. Scientific interpretation

The corpus is real, well-formed and better than expected on the time axis. It is worse
than expected on cross-layer alignment, and the specific thing it cannot do is the
thing claim C5 needs.

The honest reading is that "multi-modal" in this corpus means the two layers were
captured in parallel, not that they are joined. The descriptor does not claim
record-level correspondence; we assumed it. That assumption was ours and it was wrong,
and it is exactly the kind of assumption artefact-level verification exists to catch —
which is why the gate was written to require the download rather than the landing page.
